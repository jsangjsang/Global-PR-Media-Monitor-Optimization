# Five-minute demo guide

## Audience

Global PR leadership first; internal development second.

## Opening - 30 seconds

"This is a product concept for moving the weekly media-coverage task from manual copy-paste to exception review. It demonstrates two paths: an immediate bridge that keeps the current workflow and a production hub the internal team can build from the same foundation."

## Weekly run - 60 seconds

1. Select **Run demo import**.
2. Point out the reconciliation rail: 247 rows received, 19 duplicates grouped, 16 exceptions, and 212 records ready for Lark.
3. Emphasize that every row ends in an explicit state; nothing disappears inside an AI process.

## Human review - 60 seconds

1. Open **Review queue**.
2. Show the evidence excerpt, model confidence, product alternatives, and rules-first PIC assignment.
3. Approve the record and point to the audit trail.
4. Explain that the person reviews ambiguity rather than rebuilding every row.

## Intelligence - 60 seconds

1. Open **Coverage ledger** and inspect one record.
2. Open **Intelligence** to show product, market, sentiment, and weekly patterns.
3. Open **Monthly brief** and explain that it is an editable draft with evidence links in production, not an automatic final report.

## Operational ownership - 45 seconds

Open **Routing rules**. Explain that the PR team owns product aliases, journalist/outlet ownership, market fallbacks, and campaign overrides without requesting a code change.

## Development handoff - 45 seconds

Open **Build handoff**. Explain that Plan 1 builds the parser, rules, review behavior, and Lark-ready schema first. Plan 2 reuses those assets behind secure APIs, storage, authentication, and scheduled synchronization.

## Close - 15 seconds

"The approval requested today is a controlled Plan 1 pilot using one anonymized export, the current Lark template, product aliases, PIC rules, and a small validated history. That pilot becomes the technical specification for Plan 2."

## Important disclaimer

All placement data, outlets, journalists, metrics, article titles, record IDs, and coverage results in the demo are synthetic. No external systems are contacted.
